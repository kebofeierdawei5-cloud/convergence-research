## Research question

Can incremental capacity investment sustain high incremental ROIC while converting earnings growth into durable FCF?

The starting fact pattern is not treated as a conclusion: 2026 H1 revenue and attributable profit grew strongly, while operating cash flow grew much more slowly. The purpose of CORE-03 is to determine whether that gap is temporary working-capital/timing noise or a structural reduction in cash conversion and incremental capital efficiency.
