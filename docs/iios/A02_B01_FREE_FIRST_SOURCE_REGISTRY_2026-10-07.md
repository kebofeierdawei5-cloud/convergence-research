# A02 B-01 Free-First Source Registry — 2026-10-07

Status: PASS / ADMISSION BLOCKED

B-01 freezes a source registry that separates official public control data, route candidates, and normative rules.

A current stock list, current industry classification, or current CSI material is not accepted as historical PIT evidence merely because the page is official.

Hard free-first rules remain: no paid dependency, no Tushare prerequisite, field-level known_at, raw bytes plus metadata, and fail-closed unresolved domains.

The current blocker remains complete historical PIT materialization for ST history, industry history and source-vintage evidence across all 11 origins.

Official exchange documentation confirms that public data services cover listing, delisting, risk alerts, ST/ST removal and company-information changes; materialization and historical known_at proof remain separate admission requirements.
