## C. Response evidence requirements

When a reply or license arrives, preserve it outside the public repo first. Do not upload a private email thread or license PDF without reviewing confidentiality. Produce a sanitized rights record and retain the exact original bytes privately. The rights record must include:

- issuer / authorized licensor identity and permission instrument id;
- exact SHA-256 of the original written permission or contract bytes;
- covered exchange, data fields, symbols/universe, historical and prospective periods;
- explicit permission for private storage, local computation, internal display and backup/retention;
- restrictions on derived results, reports, frequency, retention, territory and onward sharing;
- effective date, expiry/termination and revocation terms;
- reviewer and signer roles required by the IIOS trust registry.

A general acknowledgment, delivery receipt, automated web response, or generic non-commercial website statement is not to be translated into `ALLOWED` for Exchange market data unless the written instrument clearly authorizes the specific operations required by IIOS.
